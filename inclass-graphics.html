Heat and public health

A closer look at Baltimore’s heat burden

Indoor temperatures can stay dangerous even after the sun goes down. These charts compare heat-index readings during a 2019 heat wave and heat-related death counts across Maryland from 2012 to 2018.

Key finding: Baltimore recorded 37 of Maryland’s 134 heat-related deaths from 2012 through 2018, despite having about 10% of the state’s population.
